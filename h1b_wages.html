Source & Definitions

Source: DOL Office of Foreign Labor Certification — LCA Disclosure Data FY2026 Q1 (cases received Oct–Dec 2025, released April 2026). Two files: case-level and worksite-level, joined on case number + worksite sequence.

Filtered universe: 108,529 certified + wage-plausible records out of 120,532 total (excluding withdrawn/denied and statistical outliers flagged by a plausibility check: offered wage <50% or >500% of prevailing wage).

Wage level: DOL prevailing wage levels I–IV correspond to entry (below 17th pctile), qualified (17th–34th), experienced (34th–50th), and fully competent (above 50th) relative to the OES wage distribution for the occupation/geography.

Lottery weight: Under the FY2026 wage-based lottery, each registration earns points proportional to how far the offered wage exceeds the prevailing wage. Higher-level filings at higher wages accumulate more lottery weight, improving selection odds.

Cap proxy flag: Heuristic identifying LCA filings that likely correspond to cap-subject H-1B registrations (begin date Oct 1 2025, filed Jan–Mar 2025). Not official USCIS data.

Pipeline: Python + pandas for ETL · DuckDB as analytics engine · Plotly.js for charts · GitHub Pages for hosting
