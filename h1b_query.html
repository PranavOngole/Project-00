ℹ
What this data is: Public DOL Labor Condition Application records — the salary disclosures employers must file before sponsoring an H-1B worker. What it is not: Actual payroll, lottery results, or USCIS approvals. One LCA can cover many workers. Certification does not mean the petition was approved or the lottery was won. Source: DOL OFLC FY2025 Q1–FY2026 Q1 (Oct 2024–Dec 2025), certified filings only.
📋
LCA ≠ H-1B Selected
Filing an LCA is Step 1 of 4. USCIS still has to approve the petition and (for cap-subject roles) the worker must win the lottery. Most LCAs never result in a visa.
👥
One LCA, Many Workers
A single LCA can authorize hundreds of workers for the same role. "Positions Filed" is the employer's declared headcount need — not individual H-1B applications.
⚖️
Prevailing Wage = Legal Floor
The DOL prevailing wage is the minimum the employer must offer. The wage premium shows how much above that floor — not generosity, but the compliance margin.
🏆
Level I–IV = Experience Proxy
Level I = entry/intern · Level II = qualified · Level III = experienced · Level IV = fully competent/senior. These are DOL wage bands, not job title labels.
